Research Interests

My research focuses on model evaluation, robustness, and human-centered AI. I study how models behave under changing data conditions and how evaluation can better reflect practical use.

  • Model evaluation methods that reveal failure modes before deployment.
  • Learning algorithms for robust, label-efficient, and adaptable systems.
  • Human-centered workflows for AI-assisted research, review, and decision support.
  • Responsible AI practices for transparency, safety, fairness, and accountability.
